## Reading

1. **Data scaling works exactly as the ladder predicts**: 14.6× data took
   accuracy 17.6% → 84.3% and macro F1 0.08 → 0.73.
2. **Calibration is nearly free now**: raw ECE 0.054 with temperature 1.15 —
   the model's confidence is trustworthy (v0.1 needed T=7.86, i.e. it was
   wildly overconfident at 129 examples).
3. **Confidence-gated inference can lift effective accuracy further**: with
   ECE this low, "high confidence → auto, low confidence → second opinion"
   trades coverage for accuracy favorably (lever 3 from the improvement plan).
4. **Per-class spread remains**: macro F1 0.733 vs accuracy 0.843 means
   minority/weak classes lag — class-balanced loss (lever 2) and targeted
   collection (lever 4) are the fixes, per the improvement plan.
5. Zero-shot baseline stays below random even at 236 records — the base
   model genuinely cannot do this task without fine-tuning; System-One
   fine-tuning is the right architecture bet.
