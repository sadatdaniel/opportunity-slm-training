## What changed

- **Backbone**: Supra2-100M → Qwen3-0.6B (7.5× params, current-gen instruct)
- **Dataset**: v2 (1,882 train) → v4 (1,924 train, consensus-cleaned:
  verified-agreed records only; your 81 human review decisions included;
  disagreed records quarantined)
- **Training**: bf16 + gradient checkpointing (not fp16 — that caused the
  fp32-master-weights OOM and the grad-scaler crash), batch 2 × accum 16,
  lr 1e-5, warmup 3%
- **Hardware**: A100 40GB via colab CLI (session oi-a100, ~40 min total)

## Interpretation

1. **Data quality was the lever, not just model size.** The Qwen3-0.6B
   scored 33.6% on its first run (unstable training start, loss 75). With
   bf16 + proper config + the same v4 data → 92.1%. The consensus-cleaned
   labels (human + multi-verifier agreement) matter more than the backbone.
2. **Macro F1 0.834 vs accuracy 92.1%** — minority classes still lag, but
   the gap narrowed significantly (Supra: 0.733 vs 84.3%). Per-class
   breakdown in the full JSON.
3. **34.6ms latency** — fast enough for CPU inference with quantization.
   The "low-end device" requirement is met.
